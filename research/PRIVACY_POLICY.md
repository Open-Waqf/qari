# Privacy Policy for Qari Finder

**Effective Date:** Feb 2026.

At Qari Finder, we believe your spiritual privacy is paramount. This policy explains how we handle data.

## 1. Real-Time Audio Processing

Qari Finder uses "On-Device Inference." This means:

* **No Recording:** We do not record, store, or save your audio clips.
* **No Transmission:** Your audio never leaves your device. It is processed in your browser's volatile memory (RAM) and
  discarded instantly after identification.
* **Local AI:** The "brain" of the app (TensorFlow.js) runs entirely on your hardware.

## 2. Mic Permissions

The app requires microphone access to function. This access is only active while the app is open and you are actively
seeking to identify a reciter.

## 3. Data Collection

* **Personal Data:** We do not collect names, emails, or IP addresses.

## 4. Third-Party Services

This app is hosted on https://github.com/Open-Waqf/qari. These providers may collect standard web server
logs (IP addresses, browser types), which are not controlled by Qari Finder.

## 5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
